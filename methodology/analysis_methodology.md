# Analysis Methodology
## Lotte World Busan — Visitor Sentiment Study

---

## Overview

This document describes the end-to-end analytical pipeline used to transform 887 raw
Google Maps reviews into structured business intelligence. The project combines text
mining, network analysis, and descriptive statistics.

**Tools:** KH Coder 3 · Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets  
**Dataset:** 887 Google Maps reviews, Lotte World Adventure Busan (2022–2024)  
**Languages:** Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese, and others

---

## Phase 1 — Data Collection

**Source:** Google Maps public reviews for Lotte World Adventure Busan  
**Method:** Manual collection with structured logging  
**Period:** Post-opening reviews, 2022–2024  
**Fields captured:**

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Review text | Raw visitor comment (original language) |
| Star rating | 1–5 integer score |
| Review date | Month/year of submission |
| Language | Auto-detected |

**Outcome:** 887 usable reviews after removing duplicates and non-review entries.

---

## Phase 2 — Preprocessing

Before importing into KH Coder, reviews were cleaned as follows:

1. **Language normalization** — Non-English reviews translated or flagged
2. **Noise removal** — Removed entries with no text content (rating-only reviews)
3. **Encoding** — Standardized to UTF-8 for KH Coder import
4. **Deduplication** — Removed repeated or copy-pasted reviews

No stemming or lemmatization was applied at this stage; KH Coder handles morphological
analysis internally.

---

## Phase 3 — Text Mining (KH Coder)

**Tool:** KH Coder 3 (open-source software for quantitative content analysis)

### 3a. Noun Extraction & Frequency Analysis

- Applied KH Coder's part-of-speech tagger to extract nouns from all reviews
- Generated frequency ranking of the top 100 nouns
- Top 5 keywords: **ride** (305), **good** (266), **child** (177), **fun** (161), **time** (157)

### 3b. Co-occurrence Network Analysis (CONA)

Co-occurrence analysis identifies which words appear together frequently across reviews.
A Jaccard coefficient threshold was applied to retain only statistically meaningful
word pairs.

**Four network diagrams produced:**

| File | Scope | Key insight |
|---|---|---|
| `cona_network.jpg` | All words, all reviews | 7 major semantic subgraphs |
| `cona_noun_clusters.jpg` | Nouns only, top 100 | Cleaner cluster separation |
| `rating_network_100.jpg` | Top 100 words, stratified by rating | Cross-rating word behavior |
| `rating_network_200.jpg` | Top 200 words, stratified by rating | Expanded vocabulary, finer nuance |

**Node size** = word frequency (proportional; max ~305 for "ride")  
**Node color** = subgraph / cluster membership (CONA) or degree of connection (rating networks)  
**Edge** = co-occurrence relationship above Jaccard threshold

### 3c. Seven Clusters Identified

| Cluster | Theme | Key Words |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rides & Family Experience | ride, good, child, many, long, weekday, parking, expensive |
| 2 | Park Identity & Location | BUSAN, Lotte, World, Seoul, Gyeongju, amusement, park, facility |
| 3 | Student & School Discounts | school, student, discount, card, pass, Card |
| 4 | Visitation Timing | afternoon, morning, time, people |
| 5 | Entertainment & Performances | parade, performance, fun |
| 6 | Wait Times & Crowds | ride, hour, long, weekday, weekend |
| 7 | Summer Water Rides | water, hot, summer, raincoat |

---

## Phase 4 — Keyword-Level Rating Assignment

Each high-frequency keyword was manually associated with the star-rating context
in which it most commonly appeared, based on a review-by-review reading of keyword
usage patterns.

**Process:**
1. Filter all reviews containing keyword X
2. Calculate the average star rating of those reviews
3. Assign that average as the keyword's "sentiment score"

This produced keyword-level satisfaction scores across 5 business dimensions.

---

## Phase 5 — Descriptive Statistical Analysis

**Dimensions analyzed:**

| Dimension | Keywords included |
|---|---|
| Attraction & Entertainment | ride, swing, parade, roller, performance, play, attraction, train, theme |
| Family & Kids | family, kid, baby, school, student, adult, people |
| Pricing & Discounts | discount, card, price, money, fee |
| Facilities & Services | parking, staff, rest, facility, equipment, size, area, raincoat |
| Time & Visitation | time, minute, day, hour, pm, weekday, weekend, afternoon, morning, visit |

**Statistics computed per dimension:**

- Mean (weighted by keyword frequency)
- Median
- Standard deviation
- Min / Max keyword scores

---

## Phase 6 — Business Recommendations

Recommendations were generated by triangulating:

1. Low-scoring keywords within each dimension
2. High-frequency terms in 1–2 star reviews
3. Contrast analysis between high-rating and low-rating review vocabulary

**Output:** Operational recommendations across pricing, queue management, facilities,
staffing, and scheduling.

---

## Limitations

- **Language bias:** Reviews written in Korean/Chinese/Japanese may lose nuance in
  translation; this analysis weighted English-language reviews more heavily
- **Temporal aggregation:** Reviews span 2+ years; no time-series breakdown was applied
- **Rating subjectivity:** Keyword-to-rating assignment is semi-manual and reflects
  the analysts' interpretation of context
- **Self-selection bias:** Google Maps reviewers are not a random sample of all visitors

---

## Reproducibility

The full dataset (`reviews_raw_887.xlsx`) and KH Coder project files are included in
this repository. To reproduce the CONA networks:

1. Install KH Coder 3 (free: https://khcoder.net/en/)
2. Import `reviews_raw_887.xlsx` as the corpus
3. Run: Tools → Co-occurrence Network → configure Jaccard threshold to 0.2
4. Apply subgraph coloring by community detection
